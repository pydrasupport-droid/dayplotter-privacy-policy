Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026
Last updated: 20 November 2026
In short: DayPlotter is designed to keep your tasks on your device. We do not collect, upload, sell or share your personal information through our own services. The app does not require accounts. There are no advertisements or analytics, provided the app implementation and included SDKs remain as described below.

1. Who we are

DayPlotter ("the app", "we") is an Android task planner published by KK's Studio. You can contact us at pydra.support@gmail.com.

2. Information the app stores on your device

To work, DayPlotter stores the information you enter in the app's private storage on your device, including:

  • Tasks, subtasks, notes, labels, due dates, scheduled times, reminders and repeat settings.
  • Projects, habits and their check-in history.
  • Focus timer sessions and task history, such as completed tasks.
  • Your settings, such as your optional name, working hours, theme and timer lengths.

We do not operate a server that receives this information as part of the app's normal local-storage functionality. We cannot access information stored only on your device.

3. Information we do not collect

Subject to the app's actual implementation and any included third-party SDKs:

  • The app does not require you to create an account or sign in.
  • The app does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
  • The app does not use advertising or analytics services.
  • The app does not intentionally transmit your task information to third-party tracking services.

4. Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications: To show reminders and focus timer alerts that you set. You can deny this permission and still use the app.
  • Run at startup: If enabled by the app, this allows reminders to be scheduled again after your phone restarts.
  • Vibration: To provide vibration alerts for reminders, where supported.

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Reminder information is not intentionally sent to our servers.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. If you choose Settings > Export everything, the app creates an export file that you can share using Android's sharing menu with an app or person you select. The receiving app or person may handle that file according to their own privacy practices.

6. Android backups

Depending on your device settings and the app's Android backup configuration, Android may include eligible app data in device backups associated with your Google Account. Backup availability and handling are controlled by Android, your device manufacturer and Google. Please review your device backup settings and Google's Privacy Policy for further information.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

Information stored locally remains on your device until you delete it or remove the app. Depending on the available app features, you can:

  • Delete an individual task and manage deleted tasks through the trash feature in Library.
  • Use Settings > Delete all data on this device to erase locally stored app information, if available.
  • Uninstall the app to remove its locally stored data, subject to any separate Android backups or exported files.

We do not maintain a separate server-side copy of information that remains exclusively in local app storage.

8. Security

The app stores information in its private app storage, which other ordinary apps cannot normally access. The app does not provide additional app-level encryption unless explicitly implemented. Device security, Android protections and backup settings may affect the protection of your information. We recommend using a screen lock and keeping your device updated. No storage method is completely secure.

9. Children's privacy

DayPlotter is a general-purpose productivity app and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app's local-storage functionality.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR in the European Economic Area or applicable California privacy laws, you may have rights concerning your personal information. These may include rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of your information.

Information stored exclusively on your device can generally be viewed, edited, exported or deleted using the app's available features and your device settings. If you have privacy questions, contact us at the email address provided below.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features or data practices change. Any changes will be reflected on this page along with an updated revision date. If a future feature introduces accounts, cloud sync or new data collection practices, this policy will be updated to explain those practices before their release.

12. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or DayPlotter's privacy practices, email us at pydra.support@gmail.com.
