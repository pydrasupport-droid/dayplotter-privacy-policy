Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026
Last updated: 20 November 2026
In short: Dayweave keeps your tasks on your phone. We do not collect, upload, sell or share your personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

1. Who we are

Dayweave ("the app", "we") is an Android task planner published by KK's Studio. You can contact us at pydra.support@gmail.com.

2. Information the app stores on your device

To work, Dayweave saves the following on your device only, in the app's private storage:

  • Tasks, subtasks, notes, labels, due dates, scheduled times, reminders and repeat settings that you enter.
  • Projects, habits and their check-in history.
  • Focus timer sessions and task history (for example, when you completed a task).
  • Your settings, such as your name (optional), working hours, theme and timer lengths.

This information stays on your device. We have no servers that receive it, and we cannot see it.

3. Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or files.
  • We do not use advertising, advertising IDs, analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not include third-party tracking software in the app.

4. Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications: To show reminders and focus timer alerts that you set. You can deny this permission and still use the app.
  • Run at startup: So that your reminders are scheduled again after your phone restarts.
  • Vibration: So reminders can vibrate.

All reminders are created and shown on your device. No reminder data is sent anywhere.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone. The only way data leaves the app is when you choose Settings > Export everything. This creates a file that you send using the sharing menu to an app or person you pick. What happens to that file afterward is up to you and the app you choose.

6. Android backups

Android may back up app data to your Google Account if you have device backup turned on. This is controlled by your phone settings and by Google, not by us, and it is covered by Google's Privacy Policy. You can turn backup off in your phone's system settings.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can:

  • Delete a single task, which moves it to the trash, and empty the trash from Library.
  • Erase everything with Settings > Delete all data on this device.
  • Uninstall the app, which removes its stored data from your device.

Because we do not hold copies of your data, we have nothing to delete on our side.

8. Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot normally read. The app does not add its own encryption on top of Android's, so we recommend using a screen lock and keeping your phone updated. No method of storage is completely secure.

9. Children's privacy

Dayweave is a general-purpose productivity app and is not directed at children under 13. Because the app does not collect personal information, we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the European Economic Area or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export your personal data. Since all of your data is on your own device, you can use these rights directly: view and edit it in the app, export it as a file, or delete it as described above. If you have questions, contact us at the email above.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change the app in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding cloud sync or accounts, we will update this policy and change the date at the top before the feature is released. We will tell you in the app if the change affects data you already have.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email pydra.support@gmail.com.
